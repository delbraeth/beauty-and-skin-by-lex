Questions

Good to know

Can I book Lexi now?

Yes, at the Aveda Fredric’s student salon in West Chester on Thursdays and Saturdays. Call the salon and ask for Lexi. She doesn’t do treatments outside of school until she’s licensed.

Do you see men?

Yes. Facials, peels, back treatments, body treatments and waxing are all open to men. The only exception is intimate waxing (bikini line and Brazilian), which Lexi offers to women only.

Is a student treatment safe?

Every service at the student salon is supervised by a licensed instructor. Student appointments can run a little longer than at a spa, because each step is done carefully and checked.

What does it cost?

At school, the school’s prices apply (see the menu above) and you pay the school. Prices for Lexi’s own studio will go to the waitlist before she opens.

When will her own studio open?

Lexi expects to finish school in summer 2027. Her studio opens once she passes the Ohio state exam and is licensed, in the Mason area.
